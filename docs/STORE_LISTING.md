## Full description

Tiny Talkers helps young learners practice letters and simple words in **English**, **Hindi**, and **Marathi**.

• Swipe through alphabet cards with fun emoji hints  
• Tap **Listen** to hear the letter and word  
• Tap **Say it!** and speak—the app cheers when you’re close (on supported devices)  
• Earn **stars** as you master each word—progress stays on your device  

Great for short daily practice with a parent or guardian. No account required.

Optional feedback opens in your browser. Microphone is used only when your child taps “Say it!” for pronunciation practice.
